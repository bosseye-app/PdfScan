# Privacy Policy — PDF Scan Editor

Last updated: September 11, 2026

PDF Scan Editor ("the App") is built to be completely private. All scanning, text recognition, editing, and storage happen entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect **no personal information**. The App has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Camera and Photos

Camera and photo library access is requested only so you can scan documents and import images. Everything you scan or select is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

## 3. Your Documents

All documents, scans, recognized text, tags, and settings are stored only on your device. If you enable device backups (for example iCloud), those backups are handled by Apple under your own account — we never have access to them.

## 4. Text Recognition

Text recognition runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No content is ever sent to a server.

## 5. Purchases

In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's use of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
