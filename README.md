A fully offline, on-device **PDF scanner, editor, and converter** for iPhone and iPad. Scan paper documents
and photos into PDFs, turn images into editable text with built-in OCR, then use the signature feature —
remove images and keep only text — to get a clean, lightweight, searchable text-only PDF. No account, no cloud,
no tracking: everything happens on your device.

---

## What it does

### Mobile scanner · Document scanning

Scan paper documents, photos, or whiteboards with the camera, import existing PDFs, or pick images from your
photo library to build a new scanned document. Crop, reorder, rotate, and rename pages in a clean native editor
built with SwiftUI.

### Image to text · OCR recognition

Built-in on-device OCR recognizes text in your scans and photos instantly — copyable, searchable, and shareable
— turning picture-based PDFs into genuinely editable content. Recognition runs entirely on the device, never
uploaded.

### Remove images, keep only text

One tap strips every image, photo, and background out of a PDF and keeps only the clean, readable text, producing
a smaller, lighter document. The text stays fully copyable and searchable, which makes it perfect for shrinking
file size, cleaning up old scans, and converting image-based PDFs into plain text.

### PDF editing · Format conversion

A native editing workflow: crop, rotate, reorder, and rename pages, plus advanced export options so you can
share the result to any other app.

### Document management · Tags and favorites

Tag, favorite, search, copy, and rename your documents. Titles, tags, and recognized text are all searchable, so
every scan and import lives in one tidy, filterable library.

### Offline by design · Privacy first

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your documents never leave your device — scanning, text recognition, editing,
and format conversion are 100% on-device.

### Go Pro

PDF Scan Editor Pro unlocks unlimited documents, batch text-only conversion, advanced export options, and every
premium tool.

## Typical use cases

- **Digitizing class notes**: scan handouts → OCR → remove images and keep only text, ending up with a
  searchable, copyable, far lighter text-only PDF.
- **Archiving contracts and invoices**: scan a paper contract → convert to a text PDF → tag and favorite it, then
  find it instantly by title or by keyword in the recognized text.
- **Shrinking old scans**: batch-strip images from a 40 MB picture-based PDF and watch the size collapse while
  the text stays perfectly legible.
- **Filing away whiteboards, business cards, and manuals**: snap a photo, let OCR recognize the text, and file it
  straight into the library.
- **Just need the text**: copy or share the recognized text directly into notes, email, or a chat window.
- **Checking shipping details**: scan the order page into a PDF, drop the images, keep the words, and copy the
  address in one tap.
- **Handling sensitive material**: identity documents, contracts, medical records, and receipts are processed
  without upload, without network, and without leaving a trace.

> *Built for students, researchers, and professionals who want clean, lightweight, text-only PDFs —*
> *without giving up their privacy.*
